Frequently Asked Questions
Shipping & Secure Delivery

Do you offer international shipping?
Yes, RAFAYEL & GRACE provides complimentary standard shipping worldwide. For high-value items, we utilize specialized secure courier services to ensure your pieces arrive safely and discreetly.

How long will my order take?
In-stock items are typically dispatched within 2-3 business days. Custom, bespoke, or made-to-order pieces require 4-8 weeks to allow our artisans to perfect your creation.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?
We offer complimentary returns and exchanges within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is completely unworn, in its original pristine condition, and accompanied by all original packaging, certificates of authenticity, and security tags.

In-Store Appointments

Can I book a boutique visit?
Discover the possibilities of a tailor-made visit. You can schedule an exclusive in-store appointment with one of our Client Advisors to view our collections in a private setting and discuss bespoke creations.

Signature Packaging

How will my order be packaged?
Every order is delivered in our Signature Packaging—an emblem of the House's savoir-faire. Each piece is nestled in a luxurious presentation box, elegantly ribboned, ensuring a memorable unboxing experience.

Care & Maintenance

How should I care for my fine jewelry?
We recommend cleaning your pieces gently with a soft, lint-free cloth. Avoid exposing your jewelry to harsh chemicals, perfumes, cosmetics, or extreme temperatures. To prevent scratching, always store your items individually in their original RAFAYEL & GRACE presentation boxes.
